How it works

Three steps to faster volume control

  1. 1

    Open CastVolume and tap Discover

    Allow local network access so the app can find Cast-enabled devices nearby.

  2. 2

    Select your TV or speaker

    Pick a device, pin it, and keep it ready for quick access in the app and widgets.

  3. 3

    Control volume anywhere

    Use in-app controls or widget buttons for instant volume up, down, and mute.

Privacy first

Your control stays local

CastVolume is designed for local-network control. It connects to devices on your Wi-Fi to send volume and mute commands. No account is required and no cloud dashboard is needed to get started.

  • Requires iPhone and Cast device on the same Wi-Fi network
  • Uses iOS local network permission for discovery
  • No sign-in flow to use core controls
