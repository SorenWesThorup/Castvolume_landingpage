New in 2.1

WiiM Controls widget

A new, optional widget for WiiM receivers. Start a preset, pause or resume, and nudge the volume, all from the Home Screen. The volume widgets you already use don't change.

  • Your WiiM presets, one tap each. Medium shows presets 1–2, Large shows 1–4, with their artwork.
  • Play / pause and volume. Volume down, a live volume bar, volume up and play/pause on every size.
  • WiiM Home stays in charge. Presets appear in WiiM Home's order. To change them, edit or reorder them in WiiM Home and the widget follows.
  • A WiiM page in the app. The same controls at real widget size, a device picker, and step-by-step help to add the widget.

How it works

Three steps to faster volume control

  1. 1

    Find your receivers

    Allow Local Network access and CastVolume discovers receivers on your Wi-Fi. You can also add a WiiM by IP address.

  2. 2

    Pin the rooms you use

    Pinned receivers move to the top of the app and become the targets for your widgets.

  3. 3

    Add a widget

    Choose Volume Remote, Preset Volumes or the new WiiM Controls widget, and control volume without opening the app.

Local and private

Your control stays on your Wi-Fi

CastVolume talks to your receivers over the local network to send volume, mute and playback commands. No account, no cloud service. It controls receivers; it doesn't start or stream music and isn't a universal AirPlay remote.

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.6 or later
  • iPhone and receiver on the same Wi-Fi, with Local Network access allowed
  • WiiM volume needs variable volume output. If Fixed Volume Output is on in WiiM Home, turn it off.
  • A locked iPhone may ask you to unlock before a widget command runs
